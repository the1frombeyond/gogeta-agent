# News Skill

## Description
Aggregates latest news headlines from global RSS sources. Supports filtering by category (world, technology, business), keyword search, and article summarization.

## Triggers
- latest news
- news headlines
- tech news
- search news
- what's happening

## Usage
Use headlines(category=None, region=None) for top stories, search(keyword) to find articles by topic, and summarize(url) to get a brief summary of an article.
